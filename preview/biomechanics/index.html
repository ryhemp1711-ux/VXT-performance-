Log a group training session

Save multiple athletes and reps together: continuous runs, sled pushes, wicket runs, broken runs, tempo, and 400 the hard way. Only continuous run times and splits feed race results and matching predictor inputs.

      ON-DEVICE REVIEW

      Review a wicket run

      Choose a video, mark one continuous run, and save timing with your coaching notes. Optional automatic biomechanics analysis estimates visible body positions locally. Your video is not uploaded.

      The video stays on this device. Reselect the original file after reopening a review.

      FPS is entered manually. Steps are approximate browser seeks, not guaranteed frame-accurate measurements.

      Playhead: —

      Mark the same body reference crossing each selected wicket. Use a continuous segment without cuts or frozen frames. A touchdown beyond the last wicket does not match the first-to-last distance.

      Player slow playback does not change timestamps. Without real-time confirmation, elapsed video time is shown but speed is withheld.

      Automatic biomechanics · experimental

      Use one athlete, a level side-on camera and a clear full-body view. Samples are taken about five times per second over a segment of up to 10 seconds. This does not measure ground-contact time, force, injury risk or 3D motion.
